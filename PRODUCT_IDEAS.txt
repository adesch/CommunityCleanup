COMMUNITY CLEANUP PRODUCT IDEAS
================================

CORE TERRITORY LOOP
- Territory health instead of permanent ownership.
- Suggested lifecycle: Unclaimed -> Recently Cleaned -> Healthy -> Needs Attention -> Available Again.
- Ownership could last 14-30 days and be defended with a verified new cleanup.
- Avoid unrestricted instant stealing while a block is still protected.

GAMIFICATION
- Weekly quests: clean three connected blocks, defend two streets, protect a park entrance.
- Neighborhood teams: East Downingtown, West Downingtown, schools, businesses, and community groups.
- Monthly seasons with seasonal rankings plus permanent lifetime impact statistics.
- Connected-territory bonuses for adjacent owned blocks.
- Cleanup streaks and badges: First Block, Park Protector, Downtown Defender, Weekend Warrior.
- Shareable cleanup cards with before/after photos, bags removed, blocks captured, and points earned.

COMMUNITY FEATURES
- Residents mark blocks that need cleanup, including severity, issue type, notes, and eventually photos.
- Community cleanup events with meeting point, date, supplies, target blocks, and participant check-in.
- Municipal or nonprofit notifications for reported hotspots.
- Safety escalation for needles, chemicals, dead animals, large debris, and dangerous roads.

IMPACT AND VERIFICATION
- Score verified cleanups, distance, bags, weight, and difficult hotspots instead of territory alone.
- In-app camera sessions with server timestamp, approximate GPS, and immediate upload.
- AI-assisted comparison of before/after photos.
- AI litter classification by object, material, and brand, with user confirmation.
- Cleanup history stays separate from territory ownership state.

DATA AND ADMIN
- Shared Supabase accounts, territory, reports, cleanup proof, and durable leaderboards.
- Admin review queue for disputed or suspicious cleanup proof.
- Municipality dashboard for hotspots, litter trends, events, and response status.
- CSV/open-data exports with privacy-safe location handling.

RECOMMENDED BUILD ORDER
1. Supabase authentication and shared ownership.
2. Resident cleanup-needed reports.
3. Territory health and expiration.
4. Verified cleanup records and impact scoring.
5. Weekly quests, badges, teams, and seasonal leaderboards.
